## Summary, TL; DR 总结概括

- We present **Future-KL Influenced Policy Optimization (FIPO)**, a reinforcement learning algorithm designed to overcome reasoning bottlenecks in large language models. While GRPO-style training scales effectively, it typically relies on **outcome-based rewards (ORM)** that assign a uniform advantage to every token in a trajectory.
	我们提出了一个用于突破LLM遇到的推理瓶颈的强化学习算法——受Future-KL影响的策略优化（FIPO）方法。虽然GRPO一系列的训练能够实现模型能力有效扩张，但是这种方式常常需要依靠基于结果的奖励（ORM），这意味着需要为一条轨迹的每一个Token都分配一个统一的得分。
- We argue that this **completely coarse-grained credit assignment** imposes a lower performance ceiling, as the model cannot distinguish critical reasoning steps from trivial (琐碎的) tokens.
	我们认为这种粗粒度得分分配方式会导致一个更低的能力上限，因为模型无法从琐碎的token中区分出关键的推理步骤。
- FIPO addresses this limitation by incorporating a **discounted (打折的) future-KL divergence (散度)** into the policy update, effectively establishing a **dense advantage formulation** that re-weights token-level advantages based on subsequent trajectory behavior.
	FIPO通过将打折的将来KL散度融入策略更新解决了这个缺陷，有效建立了一种根据后续轨迹行为重新对token级得分加权的细粒度得分计算公式。
- Empirically (根据经验), we find that this granular (颗粒的) reinforcement allows the model to break through the **length stagnation (停滞)** observed in standard baselines. Evaluated on **Qwen2.5-32B-Base**, FIPO extends the average chain-of-thought length from 4,000 to **over 10,000 tokens**, driving **AIME 2024 Pass@1** accuracy from 50.0% to **a peak of 58.0% .**
	根据已有经验，我们发现这种颗粒度的强化是的模型能够突破标准基线中遇到的长度停滞问题。在Owen2.5-32B-Base模型上进行评估发现，FIPO成功把平均的思维链长度从4000 token 延长到了超过10000 token，这促使AIME 2024 Pass@1准确性从50.0%上升到了最高58.0%的好成绩。
- Our results demonstrate that **establishing a dense advantage formulation is a promising direction for evolving ORM-based GRPO algorithms to unlock the reasoning potential of base models.**
	我们的研究结果表明建立一种密集的得分计算公式是一个改进基于ORM的GRPO算法来释放基础模型推理潜力的可靠方向。

## Introduction 引入

The defining shift in modern LLM development is the move toward inference-time scaling. Models like OpenAI’s o-series , Google’s Gemini and DeepSeek’s R1 have proven that by scaling Chain-of-Thought (CoT) through Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR), we can unlock elite-level (精英级) reasoning. However, the path to reproducing these results remains opaque (不透明). Most state-of-the-art recipes rely on undisclosed (未公开) algorithms and, crucially (至关重要的), a heavy dependency on large-scale, long-CoT synthetic (合成的) data to "seed" the model’s reasoning capabilities. For the community, this raises a fundamental question:

现代大语言模型发展的决定性转变是向推理时间扩展的转变。OpenAI的o系列模型、Google的Gemini和DeepSeek的R1模型等都证明了我们能够通过用有效奖励进行强化学习来扩展思维链从而达到精英级推理能力。然而，复现这些结果的途径依旧未公布。绝大多数顶尖的秘籍都依靠未公开的算法，当然更重要的是它们强烈依赖大规模、巨长的思维链合成数据来激活模型的推理能力。对于广大群众而言，这带来了一个基本问题：

> 🤔 Can we elicit deep reasoning from a base model that initially exhibits no such tendencies, without relying on long CoT synthetic data or opaque "warm-up" SFT?
> 🤔 我们能否在一个未曾表现出深度推理能力潜力的基础模型上不依靠长思维链合成数据和不透明的激活SFT操作就引导出深度推理能力。


### 问题所在

- Recent efforts like **DAPO** have successfully replicated (复制) GRPO-style training on clean base models. Yet, these models often hit a structural (系统性/结构性) ceiling (上限/瓶颈) where reasoning trajectories plateau (高原/顶峰) at intermediate lengths (~ 4,000 tokens). This is a direct consequence of **coarse-grained credit assignment**: because standard GRPO distributes a uniform advantage to every token based solely on the final answer, it fails to provide the specific signal needed for the model to evolve the complex token trajectories required for elite-level tasks.
	DAPO等近期工作已经成功在干净基础模型上复刻了GRPO风格的训练。但是，这些模型经常会栽在一个结构性瓶颈上——推理轨迹会在中等长度时（大约4000token）达到顶峰。这是粗粒度得分分配方法的直接结果：因为标准的GRPO方法仅仅根据最终的结果好坏来为一条轨迹上每个token分配一个统一的得分，它不能够提供满足精英级任务对复杂轨迹优化的足够具体的信息。
- To circumvent (规避) these sparse (稀疏) advantage signals, some frameworks revert to the PPO framework. A notable example is **Open-Reasoner-Zero**, which incorporates a value model to provide token-level feedback and succeeds in pushing reasoning lengths to the **10,000-token regime.** However, despite the added complexity of a value model, its final performance remains comparable to the more efficient, value-free **DAPO**.
	为了规避这些稀疏的奖励信号，一些框架选回了原始的PPO框架。一个典型的例子就是Open-Reasoner-Zero，它用一个价值模型提供了token级的反馈信息并成功将推理长度延长到了10000token量级。然而，除了额外添加的价值模型的复杂度，它最终的效果能够与更高效的无需价值的DAPO方法相匹敌。
- Other high-performing methods like **VC-PPO, VAPO, and T-PPO** achieve comparable and even superior results, but they rely on value models pre-trained by models that have already undergone **Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) with Long-CoT data**. Though effective, this methodology could introduce an **external knowledge prior** through the value model, creating a confounding (令人困惑的) factor (因素): it becomes difficult to discern whether the performance gains stem from the policy optimization algorithm itself or are simply inherited from the pre-trained value model.
	其他的表现优异的方法有VC-PPO、VAPO、T-PPO，这些方法在效果上能相互匹敌甚至能够有更优异的结果，但是它们都依靠用长思维链数据监督微调（SFT）所得模型进行预训练的价值模型。尽管有效，这些方法能够通过价值模型引入外部知识优势，导致了一个令人困惑的因素：我们很难去辨别这些优异的成绩到底是因为策略优化算法本身的改善还是仅仅是继承了预训练的价值模型带来的额外信息。

### 思路

To bridge this gap, we introduce **Future-KL Influenced Policy Optimization (FIPO)**. Instead of relying on a Critic to estimate token-level values, **FIPO** achieves advantage density by incorporating Future-KL divergence.

为了跨越这个鸿沟，我们引入了基于未来KL散度影响的策略优化算法。FIPO不依靠评判模型来评估token级的价值，而是通过融入未来KL散度来达到得分分配应有密度。

- By re-weighting the advantage of each token based on the cumulative (累积的) behaviors of its subsequent trajectory, **FIPO** highlights the specific tokens that drive logical progression.
	通过基于后继轨迹的累积表现重新定义每个token的得分，FIPO能够突出那些驱动逻辑进步的具体token。
- When applied to **Qwen2.5-32B-Base**, a model with no prior exposure to long-CoT data, **FIPO** effectively breaks the 4,000-token plateau of standard baselines. It enables a progressive lengthening of reasoning chains to over 10,000 tokens, pushing AIME 2024 performance to **58.0%**.
	当应用在未事先接触过长思维链数据的模型Owen2.5-32B-Base上时，FIPO方法有效打破了原来标准模型的4000token长度瓶颈。这个方法能够有效延长推理链长度到超过10000token的量级，使得AIME 2024的表现指标达到58.0%。
- By eschewing (避免) the need for a value model and starting from a vanilla base model, **FIPO** achieves performance comparable to, and in some cases superior to, these pre-trained value-model-based approaches.
	通过避免价值模型的使用并从一个干净的基础模型开始，FIPO方法的效果能够匹敌乃至在某些情况下超越那些基于预训练价值模型的强化学习方法。
- **Our result shows that establishing a dense advantage formulation is a promising direction for evolving ORM-based GRPO algorithms to unlock the inherent reasoning potential of base models**
	我们的结果表明构建一个密集型得分计算公式是一个改善基于ORM的GRPO算法以释放基础模型推理潜力的可靠方向。

## Future-KL Influenced Policy Optimization: 基于未来KL散度的策略优化算法：

### Probability Shift captures the update signal: 概率转变能捕捉更新信息：

Our method is grounded in our recent investigations into the dynamics of Large Language Models (LLMs) during reinforcement learning.

我们的方法起源于我们最近对强化学习期间大语言模型动态变化的调查。

> **Recap:**
> 
> **回顾：**
> 
> - **Sparse, Targeted Shifts**: We found that RLVR works less like rewriting the model and more like applying sparse, high-impact modifications at specific token positions in reasoning.
> 	稀疏的目标明确的转折点：我们发现RLVR发挥作用时更多是在推理过程中具体token位置做出稀疏的有巨大影响的修改而不是充写这个模型。
> - **Metrics that captures such shifts**: We found that directional (方向的) information, captured by the signed log-probability (概率值对数) difference $\Delta\log p$ between base and RLVR models, more effectively identifies reasoning-critical tokens.
> 	指标捕捉到了这些转折点：我们发现根据被标记的基础和RLVR模型之间的概率对数值差异捕捉到的方向信息能够更有效地识别关键的推理词元。
> 
> **More details see blogs:**
> 
> 更多细节详见博客：
> 
> [**On the Direction of RLVR Updates for LLM Reasoning**](https://www.notion.so/2f1b3eba1add8028be31cf1429019fed?pvs=21) 
> 
> [**Towards an Understanding of RLVR, Part I: The Ship of Theseus of Language Models**](https://www.notion.so/307b3eba1add803eb147e3ed7780a2e1?pvs=21) 

Inspired by these insights, we identify the token-level probability shift as the atomic unit for our credit assignment mechanism. Formally (正式地), we define the probability shift at time step t as the log-space difference between the current policy and the old policy:

受这些发现的启发，我们把这token级的概率变化作为我们得分分配机制的最小单元。正式地说，我们将时间步$t$时的概率变化量定义为当前策略与原始策略之间的对数空间差异：

$$
\Delta\log p(y_t|x,y_{<t}) = \log\pi_\mathrm{\theta}(y_t|x,y_{<t}) - \log \pi_\mathrm{old}(y_t|x,y_{<t}) \tag{1}
$$

> This term serves as a differential (差分的) signal capturing the instantaneous (瞬时的) policy drift (漂移):
> 
> 这一项作为一个捕捉瞬时策略漂移的差分信号：
> 
> - **Positive Shift (**$\Delta\log p_{t}$ **> 0):** Indicates that the current policy has increased the likelihood of the token relative to the old policy. This typically suggests that the training objective is **reinforcing** this specific reasoning step.
> 	正变化量（$\Delta \log p_t > 0$）：表明当前策略提高了与原始策略相关token的可能性。这通常表明训练目标是继续强化这个具体的推理步。
> - **Negative Shift (**$\Delta\log p_{t}$ **< 0):** Implies that the policy is suppressing (抑制) the generation of subsequent trajectories, signaling that the updated model is actively **down-weighting (降低权重)** this specific token relative to the reference policy
> 	负变化量（$\Delta \log p_t < 0$）：暗示了当前策略正在抑制后继轨迹的继续生成，指示更新后的模型正在积极降低这个与原始策略相关的具体token的权重。
> 
> Unlike traditional KL penalties (处罚), which treat this drift primarily as a regularization cost to be minimized, we interpret $\Delta\log p_{t}$ as a directional signal of behavioral adjustment, thereby explicitly coupling the optimization objective to the generative dynamics.
> 
> 传统的KL散度惩罚项将这种变化主要视作一个有待被减少的正则化损失，而我们将$\Delta \log p_t$视作一个调整策略的方向信号，因此能够显示将优化方向与生成的变化相匹配。

## Future-KL： 未来KL散度：

While $\Delta\log p_{t}$ captures the local distributional shift, reasoning is **inherently (固有地) a sequential (顺序的) process** where the true significance of this token depends on the trajectory it initiates (启动). To capture this causal influence, we define Future-KL as the cumulative (累积) signed probability shift from the current step t to the end of the sequence T:

尽管$\Delta \log p_t$能够捕捉当前的分布变化，推理**始终是一个有明确顺序的过程**——一个token本身真正的价值取决于它起源于哪条轨迹。为了捕捉这种因果关系，我们将未来KL散度定义为从当前时间步$t$到整个轨迹序列尾部的累积标记概率变化量：

$$
\mathrm{FutureKL}_t = \sum_{k=t}^{T} M_k \cdot\gamma^{k - t} \cdot \Delta \log p_k, \quad M_k = \mathbb{I}\left( \frac{\pi_\theta(y_k|y_{<t})}{\pi_{\text{old}}(y_k|y_{<t})} \le c \right).\tag{2}
$$

This summation (求和式) is mathematically equivalent (等效的) to the log-likelihood ratio of the joint (联合的) probability distributions for the subsequent (子) trajectory. It can thus be interpreted as a sample-based estimate of the KL divergence restricted to the future horizon, measuring the cumulative (累积的) deviation (偏差) of the current policy from the reference policy for the remainder (余数) of the trajectory. We therefore term this metric as **Future-KL**.

这个求和式在数学意义上等价于后继轨迹联合概率分布的对数似然比。因此这个式子可以被解释为一个对*受限于未来视野KL散度*的基于采样的估计，评估了当前策略相对原始策略在后继轨迹上的累积偏差。我们因此把这个指标称作**Future-KL**。

> Functionally, $\text{FutureKL}_{t}$ serves as a forward-looking metric that quantifies the cumulative shift in policy distribution regarding the future trajectory:
> 
> 在功能上来说，$\text{FutureKL}_{t}$是一个根据未来轨迹量化当前策略分布累积偏差的前瞻性指标。
> 
> - **Positive FutureKL (**$\text{FutureKL}_{t}$ **> 0):** Indicates that the updated policy has overall (总体上) **reinforced** the entire subsequent trajectory initiated by token $y_{t}$, suggesting that $y_{t}$ acts as a stable anchor for the subsequent reasoning chain. 
> 	正FutureKL（$\mathrm{FutureKL}_t >0$）：表明更新后的策略在总体上强化了自token$y_t$后整个后继轨迹。
> - **Negative FutureKL (**$\text{FutureKL}_{t}$ **< 0):** implies that the policy is collectively (总体上) **suppressing** the future tokens following $y_{t}$, signaling that the trajectory stemming from this point is becoming less favored during the optimization process.
> 	负FutureKL（$\mathrm{FutureKL_t}<0$）：表明当前策略总体上抑制了自$y_t$后的所有后继token，暗示从此点开始的后面轨迹在优化过程中开始变得不那么被看好了。
> - **Decay Window (**$\gamma^{k - t}$**):** To address the inherent uncertainty of long-horizon generation, we introduce a discount factor $\gamma \in (0,1]$ to model the **diminishing (减少的) causal dependency** between current and distant tokens. This effectively accounts for the accumulating (累积的) stochasticity (随机性) that makes future outputs increasingly less predictable over time.
> 	退化窗口（$\gamma^{k-t}$）：为了处理长视野生成固有的不确定性，我们引入了一个折扣因子$\gamma \in (0,1]$来建模当前token与远距离token之间随距离变远逐渐减少的因果依赖关系。这有效提供了累积随机性，使得未来的推断结果随着时间变长越来越不可预测。

We explicitly mask tokens that exceed the Dual-Clip threshold c. Since these tokens represent 'harmful' actions whose gradients are already clipped (via the clipped policy objective), allowing their excessively (过度的) high importance ratios to propagate (传播) into the recursive (递归) sum introduces severe variance. By zeroing out the future accumulation for these specific outliers, we remove the primary source of instability.

我们显示掩盖掉了超过了Dual-Clip阈值$c$的token。因为这些token代表那些梯度已经被裁减（通过裁剪后的策略目标）了的“有害”动作，让它们的过高权重比传播进递归求和过程将带来显著的变化。通过清零这些特殊超纲者的未来累积值，我们能够消除带来不稳定性的主要来源。

We parameterize (参数化) the decay (退化) rate as $\gamma = 2^{-\frac{1}{\tau}}$, where $\tau$ is a hyperparameter (超参数) controlling the effective horizon (or "half-life") of the future supervision. This formulation (方法) ensures that the credit assignment concentrates on the immediate reasoning chain, assigning lower weights to distant, highly uncertain tokens. Functionally,  $\tau$ defines the aperture (光圈) of this **soft decay window**.

我们把退化系数参数化为$\gamma=2^{-\frac{1}{\tau}}$，这里的$\tau$是一个用来控制未来监督有效视野（或者说是半程寿命）的超参数。这种方法保证了得分分配主要集中在即时的推理链上，将得分更少地分配给距离远的具有高度不确定性的token上。在功能上说，$\tau$定义了这个**软退化窗口**的孔径。

## Future-KL re-weighted and target loss: Future-KL 重加权和目标损失：

Finally, we integrate the temporal decay and masking mechanisms into the policy optimization objective. We propose to modulate (调节) the standard advantage estimate $\hat{A}_t$ using a future influence weight $f_t$. The modified advantage $\tilde{A}_t$ is defined as:

最后，我们将这个短期退化和掩码机制集成进了策略优化目标中。我们提出使用一个未来影响系数$f_t$来调节标准估计得分$\tilde{A_t}$。这个修正后的得分$\tilde{A_t}$被定义为：

$$
f_t = \operatorname{clip}\left( \exp(\mathrm{FutureKL}_t), \, 1 - \epsilon_{f_{low}}, \, 1 + \epsilon_{f_{high}} \right), \quad \tilde{A}_t = \hat{A}_t \cdot f_t.\tag{3}
$$

This formulation introduces two key operations:

这个公式引入了两个关键操作：

> - **Exponential Mapping:** We transform the accumulated scalar (标量) signal from log-space to a multiplicative (乘法的) domain. Mathematically, the unclipped term represents a decay-weighted (衰减加权) product of likelihood ratios, which acts as an importance weight reflecting the policy's effective preference for the generated future.
> 	$e$指数映射：我们将累积标量信号从对数域转化到了乘法域。数学意义上来说，这个未被裁减的项表示一个似然比衰减加权积，这是一个反映当前策略在生成后继轨迹上的有效表现的重要权重值。
> - **Influence Weight Clipping:** We constrain the multiplicative coefficient (系数) $f_t$ to the interval $[1-\epsilon_{f_{low}}, 1+\epsilon_{f_{high}}]$. By capping the weight, we ensure that the future trajectory modulates the update signal within a controlled range, avoiding numerical (数值) instability caused by extreme accumulated log-probability shifts.
> 	影响权重裁减：我们将乘法系数$f_t$的范围约束在$[1-\epsilon_{f_{low}}, 1+\epsilon_{f_{high}}]$内。通过为权重设限，我们保证了未来轨迹在可控范围内调节更新信号，避免因极端累积对数概率偏差值导致的数值不稳定性。

Functionally, this modulation (调制) scales the magnitude (规模) of the policy update based on the direction of the generated future. When the updated policy reinforces the subsequent trajectory ($\mathrm{FutureKL}_t > 0$), we have $f_t > 1$ and the gradient signal is magnified (被放大的). When the updated policy suppresses the subsequent trajectory ($\mathrm{FutureKL}_t < 0$), we have $f_t < 1$ and the update is attenuated.

功能上说，这个调制基于生成出的未来轨迹的方向对策略更新的规模进行了放缩。当更新后的策略强化了后继轨迹时（$\mathrm{FutureKL_t} > 0$），我们有$f_t > 1$，从而梯度信号被放大了。当更新后的策略抑制了后继轨迹时（$\mathrm{FutureKL_t < 0}$），我们有$f_t < 1$，从而更新被减弱了。

| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| $A_t>0$**(positive advantage)** | ✅ **Boost advantage**  <br>The token is treated as a stable anchor (锚点)==;== since it leads to a reinforced future, the positive signal is amplified (被放大) to encourage this path. | ⚠️ **Down-weight advantage**  <br>Down-weights the reward signal for tokens in successful paths that trigger a suppressed future. |
| $A_t<0$**(negative advantage)** | ✅ **Harsher (更严厉) penalty**  <br>The token that initiates a "preferred path" but result in failure are penalized more heavily to prevent error propagation (传播).                   | ⚠️ **Softer penalty**  <br>Attenuates (减弱) the penalty for tokens in failing paths that lead to a suppressed future.              |

| $A_t$**的值**      | $\mathrm{FutureKL}_t​>0﻿$**(强化未来)**  <br>$f_t>1$﻿ 放大更新              | $\mathrm{FutureKL}_t<0$﻿**(抑制未来)**  <br>$f_t<1$ 减弱更新 |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| $A_t>0$**(正得分)** | ✅ **提高得分**  <br>这个token被看作一个稳定的锚点==;== 因为它导向一个被强化的未来，正信号被放大来鼓励这条路径。 | ⚠️ **降低得分权重**  <br>降低成功路径中触发被抑制未来的token的奖励信号         |
| $A_t<0$**(负得分)** | ✅ **更严厉的惩罚**  <br>产生了一条“被偏好的路径”但是最终结果失败的token被更严厉地惩罚来阻止错误的传播。       | ⚠️ **更不严厉的惩罚**  <br>减弱失败路径上导向被抑制更新的未来的token的惩罚。      |

Adopting the token-level formulation from DAPO, we maximize the following FIPO objective:

从DAPO上应用这个token级的方法，我们最大化了这个FIPO目标：

$$
J_{\text{FIPO}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{(q,a)\sim \mathcal{D},\,\{o_i\}\sim \pi_{\theta_{\text{old}}}} \left[ \frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^{G}|o_i|} \sum_{i=1}^{G}\sum_{t=1}^{|o_i|} \min \left( r_{i,t} f_{i,t} \hat{A}_{i,t}, \, \operatorname{clip}\left(r_{i,t}, 1-\epsilon, 1+\epsilon\right) f_{i,t} \hat{A}_{i,t} \right) \right] \tag{4}
$$
