# Preparation Checklist for Research Opportunities

## My Judgment at the Time

At that point, I knew it would be hard to create a large gap through grades alone, while my research experience was still close to zero. That meant the key was not waiting for opportunities, but preparing myself to be ready for them.

## What I Prepared

### Materials

- Condensed my resume into a one-page version.
- Extracted the most representative parts of course projects.
- Prepared a short but clear self-introduction.

### Information Gathering

- Read faculty webpages and group descriptions.
- Checked whether the direction matched my longer-term interests.
- Looked at recent papers from each group before reaching out.

## Looking Back

What usually matters is not simply whether I was already strong enough, but whether an advisor could see that I was willing to learn, able to stay engaged, and genuinely interested in the direction.
